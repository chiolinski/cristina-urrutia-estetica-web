## Prompt 1

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait hero image for a premium Chilean orofacial aesthetics website. Create a refined editorial still life: an ivory stone sculptural female face in three-quarter profile, abstract yet delicate, shoulders and neck suggested, soft organic folded stone shapes behind it. Warm champagne and ivory palette, subtle natural stone texture, soft window light, deep gentle shadows, luxury European beauty editorial art direction. Vertical composition, sculpture fills central area, sufficient margin for arch-shaped crop. Clearly an artwork, not a photograph of a real doctor or patient. No medical equipment, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Save image output for website integration.

## Prompt 2

A tactile ivory plaster relief artwork of a mature female face in calm profile, with delicate lips, nose and cheek contours. Beside the relief, a single curved carved stone shape and subtle linen folds. Still-life fine art photograph, entirely sculptural, no real people. Warm ivory, sand, champagne, taupe; soft natural side light, understated beauty editorial, no gloss, no medical equipment, no products, no text, no watermarks. Landscape composition with face on right half and organic shapes on left. Clearly illustrative, never a clinical result.
